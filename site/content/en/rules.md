## The General Handbook governs

CallingSupportApp is a tool serving Church units, not a place to invent new practices. Every feature complies with the [General Handbook](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook?lang=eng). If something conflicts with it, it is not built; and if the application and the Handbook appear to say different things, **the Handbook wins**.

## For leaders who use the application

- **Each person has their own account.** Users and passwords are not shared, just as the Handbook requires for Church accounts (33.9.1.1).
- **Turn on two-step verification.** The Handbook “strongly recommends” enabling it for Church accounts (33.9.1.1), and the same standard applies here.
- **No data on shared computers** (33.9.1.2). Do not check “Remember my session” on someone else’s computer, and delete lists you download or print when you no longer need them.
- **Use data only for the activity** for which it was provided, never for personal, political, or commercial purposes ([33.8](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/33-records-and-reports?lang=eng)).
- **Do not import lists** from official Church systems.

## Money

The application **does not handle money**. According to [chapter 34](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/34-finances-and-audits?lang=eng), “Only the bishop and his counselors may receive tithes and other offerings” (34.5.2), and “Generally, members should not have to pay to participate in activities” (34.6.2.2). The application shows an estimated cost and how to contribute a donation in the category designated by the bishopric, online or with the form. **Leaders do not receive cash.**

*Quotes from the General Handbook are translated from the Spanish text; the official English text is at the links.*

## Activities

- **Temple trip:** “All ward and stake temple trips should be scheduled with the temple” ([25.1.2](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/25-temple-and-family-history-work?lang=eng)). A trip cannot be activated without confirmation.
- **Camps** ([chapter 20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/20-activities?lang=eng)):
  - at least two adults must be present (20.7.1);
  - each participant provides the signed official “Permission and Authorization for Medical Care” form (20.7.4);
  - a participant contribution is requested only if the bishopric authorizes it because the budget is insufficient (20.6.2).

## Business directory

The directory is **disabled** by default and is activated only with the bishopric’s approval, because the [General Handbook, 38.8.5](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/38-church-policies-and-guidelines?lang=eng) says: “Church meetinghouses and other properties, Church meetings and classes, and Church websites and social media channels should not be used to promote businesses or entities that are not owned by the Church.”

Anyone who publishes accepts a responsibility: every member should help **“safeguard the purity, integrity, and good name of the Church”** (a phrase from [lesson 107 in the Seminary manual on Doctrine and Covenants 102](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/doctrine-and-covenants-and-church-history-seminary-teacher-manual-2014/section-5/lesson-107-doctrine-and-covenants-102?lang=eng)). In practice:

- only truthful information;
- nothing that harms another person’s reputation (which is why there are no reviews or comments);
- nothing that suggests the Church or the unit endorses a business;
- the directory is not promoted in Church meetings, classes, or channels.

## If you see misuse

The Handbook asks that “misuse of Church information, including personal data,” be reported (33.9.1). Speak with your unit leaders. If it is a software issue, report it as explained in [Security](../security/).
