CallingSupportApp is made by **CSATeam (Calling Support App Team)**, a community of volunteer contributors. **We are all contributors and no one is above anyone else:** there is no creator, owner, or project leader. Decisions are made in issues and, above all, the [General Handbook](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook?lang=eng) governs.
