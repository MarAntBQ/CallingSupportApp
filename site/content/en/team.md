CallingSupportApp is made by **CSATeam (Calling Support App Team)**, a community of volunteer contributors. **We are all contributors and no one is above anyone else:** there is no creator, owner, or project leader. Decisions are made in issues and, above all, the [General Handbook](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook?lang=eng) governs.

## Join us

**Anyone, whether a member or a friend of the Church, can take part without asking for permission.** You fork the repository, open a PR, and if it is approved, your work becomes part of the project:

1. Read [how to contribute](https://github.com/MarAntBQ/CallingSupportApp/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
2. Add your profile in [`team/`](https://github.com/MarAntBQ/CallingSupportApp/tree/main/team): it is your first contribution.
3. Choose an available issue and comment `/tomar`.

## Official contributors

Being an official contributor gives you write access to the repository. **It is not a rank:** no one is above anyone else, and the rules are the same for everyone. There are two paths:

1. **By invitation** from someone on the team.
2. **By contributing in the open:** after several merged PRs, the team invites you to join.

The invitation is proposed in a public issue and, if no one raises a well-founded objection within a week, it is sent.
