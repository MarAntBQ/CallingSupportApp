## What is next

1. **Foundation:** skeleton, languages, sessions, configuration, email, users, and permissions.
2. **Temple trip:** the complete module from the first version.
3. **Ready for other wards:** installation guide, data purge, and two-step verification.
4. **New modules:** camp, EnglishConnect, and self-reliance.

Each step is an issue that any contributor can take: [see available issues](https://github.com/MarAntBQ/CallingSupportApp/issues?q=is%3Aopen+is%3Aissue+no%3Aassignee+-label%3Abloqueado).
