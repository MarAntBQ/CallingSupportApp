## Before you start

- Approval from your bishopric to use the application in the unit.
- A free GitHub, Vercel, and Supabase account.
- Who will be the **data steward** for your installation.

## When it is ready, the steps will be

1. Create the database in Supabase.
2. Deploy the application to Vercel with the "Deploy" button.
3. Open `/setup` and create the first administrator account.
4. In **Settings**: unit name, logo, default language, and data steward.
5. Create the organizations and callings, and grant module permissions.

This manual will be completed step by step, with screenshots, as those issues are closed.
