## The idea

In CallingSupportApp, **permissions belong to callings, not people**. If the ward clerk has permission to view the Temple trip, whoever receives that calling will have it too, and whoever no longer has it loses access immediately.

## What you will be able to do

- Create your unit’s organizations and callings.
- Give each calling permission to read, create, edit, or delete in each module.
- Choose who receives notices from each module.
- Invite someone: they receive an email to create their password; passwords are never sent by email.
