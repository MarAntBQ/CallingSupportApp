## For people registering

1. Open the trip link shared by your unit.
2. Enter your information and your family’s; the form shows which ordinances and services have slots.
3. Read the data notice and check your consent.
4. If you want to contribute to expenses, make a donation in the category designated by your bishopric, online or with the form. **Leaders do not receive cash**, and participation does not depend on payment.

## For organizers

- Create the trip **after scheduling it with the temple** (General Handbook 25.1.2).
- Define dates, services, estimated costs, and slots by ordinance and gender.
- Approve registrants, assign rooms, and print the lists for the day of the trip.
