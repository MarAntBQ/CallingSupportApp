CallingSupportApp is made by **CSATeam (Calling Support App Team)**. We are all contributors and no one is above anyone else: there is no creator, owner, or project leader.

## What we expect

- **Respect and kindness** toward everyone, in issues, PRs, comments, and messages.
- **Honesty:** we say what we verified and how. A PR that marks something as tested when it was not tested is a document that lies.
- **Written and public decisions:** they are discussed in issues, with reasons. Above all, the [General Handbook](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook?lang=eng) governs.
- **Care for people:** we never publish real member data, whether in code, screenshots, or issues.
- **Patience:** we are volunteers and each person contributes in their own time.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, mockery, or offensive language.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing other people’s personal data.
- Using the project for self-promotion, business, or politics.

## What happens if it occurs

Repository maintainers may hide comments, close conversations, or block access for anyone who does not respect this code, always explaining why. If something makes you uncomfortable, write to [devteam@callingsupportapp.org](mailto:devteam@callingsupportapp.org): the message will be handled discreetly.
