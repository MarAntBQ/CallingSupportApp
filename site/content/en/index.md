CallingSupportApp is a **free and open source** application that helps ward or branch leaders organize activities —the temple trip, camps, EnglishConnect classes, self-reliance— with registrations, slots, lists, and notices, while protecting member data. It is made by **CSATeam**, a team of volunteer contributors.

> **It is not an official site** of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints; official information is at [churchofjesuschrist.org](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org). **It does not replace Member Tools**, the official app: it only adds functionality that does not currently exist.

The entire project is governed by the [General Handbook](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook?lang=eng). If an idea conflicts with it, it is not built.
