- The repository is **public** and anyone can contribute.
- Each issue is **self-contained**: it includes everything needed to work on it without asking questions.
- To take an issue, comment **`/tomar`**: a bot assigns it and notifies everyone else. Issues without activity are released automatically.
- **CSATeam** is born: a team of equal contributors; each person adds their own profile.
- The Church’s **General Handbook** becomes the governing rule for every project idea.
