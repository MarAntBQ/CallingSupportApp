Write to us at **[church@marantbq.dev](mailto:church@marantbq.dev)**.

## What to write about

- Questions about the project or how to install it in your ward.
- Interest in contributing (code, translations, testing, ideas).
- A security problem (see [Security](../security/)).
- Something that does not comply with this [code of conduct](../conduct/).

## What not to write about

- **Data from a specific installation:** it is kept by the unit using it; write to its leaders.
- **Church matters, your membership record number, or Member Tools:** speak with your unit leaders or use the official channels at [churchofjesuschrist.org](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org).

For software proposals and bugs, the best option is to [open an issue](https://github.com/MarAntBQ/CallingSupportApp/issues/new/choose): that keeps it visible to the whole team.
